CABCIN - Co-funded by the European Union

Establishment of Capacity Building Centers

CABCIN is a project co-funded by the European Union under Erasmus+ Action 2 - focused on building capacity centres for Higher Educational Institutions. Parul University was among the lead Indian partners and successfully completed the project, establishing Capacity Building Centres to raise teaching staff standards across Indian HEIs.
