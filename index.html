Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

What the Centre for International Relations & Research works towards, every day.

Our Vision

To make global exposure a defining part of every Parul University education - establishing CIRR as a trusted gateway that connects our students and faculty to leading universities worldwide, and welcomes the world to Vadodara.

Our Mission

Build sustainable academic partnerships across continents. Create accessible mobility routes for Parul students. Support every student end-to-end from application to arrival. Bring global perspectives onto campus through inbound students, visiting faculty and international events.

Director's Message

A word from our Director.

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to the global learning ecosystem of Parul University. At the Center for International Relations and Research, we believe that education must transcend borders, broaden perspectives, and foster global citizenship.

Over the years, I have witnessed firsthand the transformative power of international exchange - not just in academics, but in the hearts and minds of every student who dares to explore beyond their comfort zone. Through our partnerships with leading universities across the world, we have built bridges of learning that allow our students to study abroad, collaborate on global research, and immerse themselves in culturally diverse environments.

Dr. Preeti Nair

Director, International Relations
